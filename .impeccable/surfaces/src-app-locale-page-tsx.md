## Direction contract

THESIS: A dark stage crossed by one stream of light, the data Mikhail moves for a living (100 000 objects a day). The stream runs through the whole page in the right margin and dives in at three moments: past the portrait, through the stack, into the call button. Visuals stand in the open on the stage; the text stays calm and factual. Refuses neon cyan glow, glass panels, bento cards, boxed visuals and gradient text.

OWN-WORLD: Theatre black #121110 with a warm cast, off-white type, warm grey secondary text, hairline rules. One amber light (#f2b45a): the stream, the primary action and the single key fact of a section. Dust of data in depth over every page. Geologica 800 for display, 400/500 for text, JetBrains Mono only for code. No frames around visuals.

STORY: The recruiter reads role, stack, format and contacts in the first screen and writes. The lead watches three company chapters with real evidence (before/after, markets globe, the night city of lit windows, a live repricing board, quotes), the stack assembling, and the library itself.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The name gathers out of streaks of light (assemble, dashes) at display scale. Under it the role line, the lead, the actions «Написать в Telegram», phone and CV, then a line about Diorama. The portrait on the right in an amber duotone that shows colour under the pointer; the stream pours in from the top right and runs down the portrait's left edge in front of it, never across the face. A row of four static key numbers closes the first screen at 1440×900. Phone: name, role, lead and actions inside the first screen.

FORM: owner-pinned "data stream" world on top of the pinned "Тёмная сцена"; seed: none (owner-pinned; prior roll seed:d672ff7585ae1ec6). Craft bar: the dark product pages of Apple, Linear and Vercel, and the owner's own landings dioramajs.dev and intouchping.com.
